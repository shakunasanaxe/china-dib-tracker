03 — Operational Realities

Case Studies & Battlefield Post-Mortems

Training and combat deployment reveal genuine issues — from mechanical failures to absent after-sales support

3.1 — Southeast Asia
🇹🇭
Thailand
December 2025
Critical
VT-4 Gun Barrel Explosion Near Cambodia Border
VT-4 MBT • NORINCO
During a live-fire exercise, a 125mm gun barrel exploded while firing HE ammunition. All three crew severely wounded. Thai tank crews also report melting barrels past "safe limits" and underperforming V12 diesel engines. Cambodia has damaged three Thai VT-4s in border clashes.
History
Thailand's 1970s Type 69 MBTs sat unused for decades, then were dumped into the ocean as artificial reefs. The Thai Ordnance Department attributes the 2025 explosion to "thermal stress."
🇲🇲
Myanmar
Nov 2022 – 2025
High
JF-17 Fleet Grounded; FTC-2000Gs Shot Down
JF-17, FTC-2000G • AVIC
Majority of JF-17 fleet grounded due to structural airframe cracks, CETC KLJ-7A radar failures, and inability to perform BVR targeting. Pakistani technicians secretly deployed to Pathein airbase. Two FTC-2000Gs confirmed shot down — one by a Chinese-made FN-6 MANPAD, ironically.
Systemic Issue
Myanmar lacks technical expertise for repairs and cannot import Western spares due to sanctions. Radar resolution-cell issues with KLJ-7/7A affect situational awareness in dense combat environments.
3.2 — West Asia
🇮🇶
Iraq & Jordan
2016–2021
High
CH-4B Fleets: Mass Crashes, Grounded, Sold Off
CH-4B Rainbow • CASC
Eight of Iraq's 20 CH-4Bs crashed from technical malfunctions; the rest grounded by 2021 due to absolute lack of spares from CASC. Jordan put all 6 CH-4Bs up for sale within three years of acquisition. Algeria also experienced multiple crashes during routine drills.
Broader Pattern
Despite failures, Iraq has since purchased CH-5 drones. Baghdad unveiled one during a live missile drill as US forces exited Ain Al-Asad in February 2026. Countries continue engagement despite evidence.
🇸🇦
Saudi Arabia
March 2026
Continuing
$5B Wing Loong-3 Factory Despite Operational Concerns
Wing Loong III • AVIC
Riyadh signed a landmark co-production deal despite known operational issues with earlier Chinese drones in the region. Driven by desire to diversify from US, strategic pricing (1/4 of MQ-9B cost), and Vision 2030 indigenous capability goals. Saudi is the top UCAV buyer (~85 units, 2015–25).
3.3 — Africa
🇳🇬
Nigeria
2020–2025
Critical
VT-4 "Temu Tanks": 30-Min Cooldowns, Destroyed by RPGs
VT-4 MBT, F-7 • NORINCO, AVIC
Procured for $152M to fight Boko Haram (Operation Tura Takai Bango). Turrets required 30-minute cooldowns between engagements. Boko Haram destroyed multiple VT-4s with simple RPGs. Nigeria also lost 3 F-7 jets to crashes; returned 7 of 9 remaining to China.
Diversification
Nigeria resumed negotiations with Russia for T-90 MBTs ($99.5M). Armed forces continue using Czech/Ukrainian T-72s and British Vickers Mk-3 tanks instead.
🇰🇪
Kenya
2016–present
Critical
VN-4 APCs: Chinese Reps Refused to Sit Inside During Tests
VN-4 APC • NORINCO / Chongqing Tiema
Chinese sales representatives from Chongqing Tiema Industries refused to sit inside VN-4s during test firings. Kenya's National Police Service found APCs "cannot withstand an RPG attack." IEDs planted by Al Shabaab caused injuries and deaths. Kenya signed deals for South African Springbuck APCs and US M1117s in late 2025.
3.4 — Venezuela & Pakistan
🇻🇪
Venezuela
January 2026
Critical
Total Radar Blindness During US Op. Absolute Resolve
JY-27A, JYL-1 Radars • CETC
US Joint Chiefs assessed that Chinese radars "failed to detect US fighter jets or aircraft." $2B+ layered air defence network (Chinese radars + Russian S-300s/Pantsir) did not fire a single shot. Whether systems were overwhelmed by EW, internally sabotaged, or simply broken is debated — but 60%+ of the radar network was already non-operational due to years of spare parts shortages.
🇵🇰
Pakistan
May 2025 & ongoing
High
Operation Sindoor Exposes Chinese Air Defence & Naval Gaps
HQ-9P, HQ-16, JF-17, F-22P Frigates • Multiple SOEs
Indian airstrikes penetrated defences heavily reliant on Chinese SAMs. F-22P frigate FM-90(N) missile systems exhibit faulty image processing and cannot lock targets; IR17 sensors and SR60 radars also defective. Multiple JF-17s have crashed during drills. Yet Pakistan continues procuring — Type 054A/P frigates, Hangor subs, CM-401 hypersonic missiles.

04 — Outlook

Concerns & The Future

Three structural headwinds, internal purges, and the after-sales vacuum

01
Post-Ukraine Resurgence of Russian & US Exporters
China's rise to 4th-largest exporter was partly due to Russia's shrinking share during the Ukraine war, inflating Beijing's contribution numbers. As Russian and American production lines ramp back up, market share competition will intensify — especially in Africa and West Asia.
02
Widening Net of US BIS & 1260H Designations
The June 2026 list now includes ~188 entities, including commercial giants like Alibaba, Baidu, and BYD. Compliance scrutiny extends globally under the "small yard, high fences" strategy, complicating supply chains for China's dual-use components and non-traditional vendors.
03
After-Sales Vacuum & Buyer's Remorse
Once a Chinese SOE secures the initial sale, it does not address the remaining life cycle. No spares, no training, no maintenance logistics. As this becomes a bone of contention even for continuing clients, deeply intertwined defence ties like those binding China to Pakistan remain the exception, not the rule.
Anti-Corruption Purges Across the MIC (2023–26)
Internal investigations into procurement rigging, kickbacks, and misreporting have led to the removal of top leadership across the CMC, Rocket Force, and major defence SOEs.
