01 — Structure

The Defence Industrial Base

Key state-owned enterprises, Military-Civil Fusion architecture, and the platforms they produce

China's DIB is anchored by a constellation of massive state-owned enterprises (SOEs) operating under the direct oversight of the Central Military Commission (CMC) and the State Council's State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission (SASAC).

The overarching strategy binding civilian tech and military production is Military-Civil Fusion (MCF), a whole-of-society mandate enshrined in the 13th, 14th, and 15th Five-Year Plans. MCF aims to channel dual-use advances in AI, quantum computing, semiconductors, and metamaterials into PLA modernisation.

According to SIPRI, 11 state-owned enterprises hold exclusive, permanent authorisation to export conventional arms—private companies and citizens are prohibited. Four of these (NORINCO, Poly Technologies, Jing'an, Xinxing) are authorised for SALW; two more (CPMIEC, ALIT) handle MANPADS. Recent PLA procurement data also reveals a surge in Non-Traditional Vendors (NTVs)—private firms with no formal state ownership—securing ~764 AI-related defence contracts between 2023–2024, complicating Western sanctions frameworks.

03 — Quality

Quality Control Failure Tracker

Documented battlefield failures, consumer complaints, and systemic defects reported by importing states

A widening gap has emerged between the theoretical capabilities of Chinese military hardware and its operational reality under combat stress. From the collapse of Venezuelan air defences in January 2026, to the HQ-9B debacle in Iran, to chronic armoured vehicle failures in Africa—the Chinese defence export portfolio is increasingly characterised by critical technical failures and absent after-sales support.

Analysts attribute this to a DIB fundamentally reliant on reverse engineering—capable of replicating exterior architecture but unable to master the metallurgy, materials science, and software integration required for extreme operational stress. The "after-sales vacuum" (no lifecycle support, no maintenance logistics, no spare parts) accelerates platform degradation across all theatres.
