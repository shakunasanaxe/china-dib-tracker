03 — Operational Realities

Case Studies & Battlefield Post-Mortems

Training and combat deployment reveal genuine issues — from mechanical failures to absent after-sales support

3.1 — Southeast Asia
🇹🇭
Thailand
December 2025
Critical
VT-4 Gun Barrel Explosion Near Cambodia Border
VT-4 MBT · NORINCO
During a live-fire exercise, a 125mm gun barrel exploded while firing HE ammunition. All three crew severely wounded. Thai tank crews also report melting barrels past "safe limits" and underperforming V12 diesel engines.
History
Thailand's 1970s Type 69 MBTs sat unused for decades, then were dumped into the ocean as artificial reefs. The Thai Ordnance Department attributes the 2025 explosion to "thermal stress."
🇲🇲
Myanmar
Nov 2022 – 2025
High
JF-17 Fleet Grounded; FTC-2000Gs Shot Down
JF-17, FTC-2000G · AVIC
Majority of JF-17 fleet grounded due to structural airframe cracks, CETC KLJ-7A radar failures, and inability to perform BVR targeting. Two FTC-2000Gs confirmed shot down — one by a Chinese-made FN-6 MANPAD.
Systemic Issue
Myanmar lacks technical expertise for repairs and cannot import Western spares due to sanctions. Pakistani technicians secretly deployed to Pathein airbase.
3.2 — West Asia
🇮🇶
Iraq & Jordan
2016–2021
High
CH-4B Fleets: Mass Crashes, Grounded, Sold Off
CH-4B Rainbow · CASC
Eight of Iraq's 20 CH-4Bs crashed from technical malfunctions; the rest grounded by 2021 due to absolute lack of spares. Jordan put all 6 CH-4Bs up for sale within three years of acquisition.

Continuing
$5B Wing Loong-3 Factory Despite Operational Concerns
Wing Loong III · AVIC
Riyadh signed a landmark co-production deal despite known operational issues with earlier Chinese drones. Driven by desire to diversify from US, strategic pricing, and Vision 2030 goals.
3.3 — Africa
🇳🇬
Nigeria
2020–2025
Critical
VT-4 "Temu Tanks": 30-Min Cooldowns, Destroyed by RPGs
VT-4 MBT, F-7 · NORINCO, AVIC
Procured for $152M to fight Boko Haram. Turrets required 30-minute cooldowns. Boko Haram destroyed multiple VT-4s with simple RPGs. Nigeria also lost 3 F-7 jets to crashes; returned 7 of 9 remaining to China.
Diversification
Nigeria resumed negotiations with Russia for T-90 MBTs. Armed forces continue using Czech/Ukrainian T-72s and British Vickers Mk-3 tanks instead.
🇰🇪
Kenya
2016–present
Critical
VN-4 APCs: Chinese Reps Refused to Sit Inside During Tests
VN-4 APC · NORINCO / Chongqing Tiema
Chinese sales representatives refused to sit inside VN-4s during test firings. Kenya's National Police Service found APCs "cannot withstand an RPG attack." Kenya signed deals for South African Springbuck APCs and US M1117s in late 2025.

04 — Outlook

Concerns & The Future

Three structural headwinds, internal purges, and the after-sales vacuum

01
Post-Ukraine Resurgence of Russian & US Exporters
China's rise to 4th-largest exporter was partly due to Russia's shrinking share during the Ukraine war, inflating Beijing's contribution numbers. As production lines ramp back up, market share competition will intensify.
02
Widening Net of US BIS & 1260H Designations
The June 2026 list now includes ~188 entities, including Alibaba, Baidu, and BYD. Compliance scrutiny extends globally under the "small yard, high fences" strategy.
03
After-Sales Vacuum & Buyer's Remorse
Once a Chinese SOE secures the initial sale, it does not address the remaining life cycle. No spares, no training, no maintenance logistics. Deeply intertwined ties like those binding China to Pakistan remain the exception.
Anti-Corruption Purges Across the MIC (2023–26)
Internal investigations into procurement rigging, kickbacks, and misreporting have led to the removal of top leadership across the CMC, Rocket Force, and major defence SOEs.
